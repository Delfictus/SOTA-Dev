# Enterprise Positioning Summary

PRISM is **an immutable pharmacological evidence environment for receptor-state transition analysis under non-equilibrium perturbation**.

## Evidence Boundary

The platform partitions claims into epistemic classes before they enter reports, visualizers, or CRO handoffs. Observed tensors and deterministic transforms remain distinct from projected assay behavior and hypothesized chemistry.

| Epistemic class | Level | Definition |
|---|---:|---|
| OBSERVED | 1 | Direct tensor-derived measurement present in simulation output. |
| DERIVED | 2 | Deterministic mathematical transform of observed tensors. |
| INFERRED | 3 | Mechanistic interpretation derived from multi-tensor convergence. |
| PROJECTED | 4 | Translational extrapolation into expected assay behavior. |
| HYPOTHESIZED | 5 | Biological interpretation requiring experimental falsification. |

## Translation Rule
